# What the Assistant Can Do

This document describes what the Roche Scientist Assistant can help with
today. It is the canonical reference when a scientist asks "what can you
do?" or "how can you help me?".

## Answering Operational Questions

The assistant answers questions grounded in internal lab documentation.
Common topics include:

- Onboarding and access requests — which portal to use, how to request
  access to a lab application, and what training is required first.
- Navigating internal applications — which tool covers which task and
  how to reach it from the Application Catalog.
- Incident reporting — when to open a ServiceNow incident and what
  information to include.
- Booking laboratory instruments — finding availability, reserving a
  slot, modifying or cancelling.
- Checking sample stock — reading on-hand versus available quantities,
  reserving stock, and requesting restocks.
- Cleaning laboratory devices — recommended cleaning agents for
  centrifuges, pipettes, microscopes, and computer surfaces.
- Decontamination procedures — containment, PPE, agent selection, and
  recording the event.
- Virtual session troubleshooting — login problems, disconnects,
  performance issues, and saving work.

Every answer is grounded in the source documentation, and the assistant
cites the document and section it drew from.

## Pointing You to the Right Internal App

When the action you want to take lives inside another internal Roche
application, the assistant explains which application to use and how to
get there. The assistant does not perform the action inside that
application on your behalf.

## Recording Feedback for IT

When you tell the assistant that something is confusing, frustrating,
or could be improved, it recognises the message as feedback rather than
a question and stores it for the IT and documentation teams. The
assistant also detects the dominant emotion in the feedback so that the
teams can prioritize what scientists find most painful.

## Speaking Your Language

The assistant detects the language you write in and replies in the same
language. Supported languages are English, German, French, and Italian.
The source documentation is in English; the assistant translates its
answers into your language automatically.

## Remembering the Conversation Across Devices

Your chat history is tied to your session, not to a specific device.
When you move between lab devices, the assistant continues the
conversation from where you left off.

## What the Assistant Cannot Do Yet

A few features are planned but not yet available:

- Creating ServiceNow incidents directly from the conversation.
- Reading documents from Google Drive — the current corpus is a curated
  local set.
- Performing actions inside other internal Roche applications, such as
  booking an instrument or reserving stock on your behalf.

If you ask the assistant to do one of these, it will explain the manual
steps to take instead.
