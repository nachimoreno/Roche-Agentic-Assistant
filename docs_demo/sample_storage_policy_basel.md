# Sample Storage Policy — Basel Site

## Storage temperature and duration

At the Basel site, blood serum samples must be stored at **-20 °C** and may be
kept for **up to 30 days** before they must be disposed of. Do not store serum
samples at -80 °C: at this site, repeated deep-freezing is considered to degrade
the analytes and is not permitted.

Label every tube with the collection date so the 30-day limit can be tracked.
