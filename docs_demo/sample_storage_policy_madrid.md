# Sample Storage Policy — Madrid Site

## Storage temperature and duration

At the Madrid site, blood serum samples must be stored at **-80 °C** and may be
kept for **up to 7 days** before they must be disposed of. Storing serum samples
at -20 °C is not permitted at this site, as it is considered to compromise
sample integrity.

Record the freezer location and the collection date for every sample.
